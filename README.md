### 6. MAP & CADASTRAL MEASUREMENT TOOL

- **Leaflet Map:** Centered on Azare with Street and Satellite view switches. Disable scroll-wheel zooming on page scroll.

- **Interactive Boundary Drawer:** Numbered corner pins with edge measurements shown in both feet and metres.

- **Area Maths:** Real-time square metres, square feet, and standard 50×100ft plots calculation.

- **Cadastral Notice:** Always display: *"Estimated Map Boundary Calculation — For indicative planning only. Not a certified cadastral survey."*

- **GPS Walk Mode:** Live phone GPS tracking with Start, Pause, Resume, and Finish buttons to record real corner points while walking the plot.

- **Printable A4 Sales Receipts:** Branded G10 receipts with receipt numbers (`G10-RCP-YYYY-####`), buyer name & phone, plot details, payment method (Full or Easy Buy installment), QR code, and dual sign-off.
